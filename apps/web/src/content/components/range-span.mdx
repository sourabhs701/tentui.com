Range Span is a React two-handle range slider for shadcn/ui with a stretchy
selected fill, draggable span panning, and live min and max value labels that
merge automatically when handles converge. TentUI is free and source-available:
drag either thumb, pan the selected bar as one interval, pull past either limit
for rubber-band feedback, or step through values with a keyboard.

## Features

- Stretches like rubber past `min` and `max`: dragging a handle or panning the
  selected span past either limit extends the track and fill with square-root
  resistance up to `maxStretch` px, then springs back cleanly on release.
- Pushes back softly at `minGap`: pulling one handle into the other applies a
  subtle damped resistance nudge and muted thunk instead of feeling frozen.
- Drags the whole interval at once: with `draggableSpan` (on by default), grab
  the selected fill between handles to pan the `[min, max]` window while keeping
  its width constant.
- Keeps live min and max labels legible at every width: `ResizeObserver` tracks
  both floating pills, clamps them inside the track bounds, and merges them into
  one centered `min – max` pill with hysteresis before they ever collide.
- Handles overlapped thumbs cleanly: when `low === high` (even at `0` or `100`),
  dragging left immediately claims the lower handle and dragging right claims
  the upper handle so neither thumb can ever get trapped.
- Drives handles, fill `clip-path`, track stretch, and pill coordinates through
  `motion/react` `MotionValue`s on GPU-composited transforms, rolling unformatted
  digits with NumberFlow and playing optional tiks sound and haptics.

## Usage

```tsx
import { RangeSpan } from "@/components/range-span";
```

```tsx
<RangeSpan
  label="Price range"
  min={0}
  max={600}
  step={10}
  defaultValue={[140, 440]}
  format={(value) => `$${value}`}
/>
```

### Controlled state

Pass a `[min, max]` tuple to `value` along with `onValueChange` to drive the
range from React state or preset buttons. External updates glide smoothly on a
spring, while `onValueCommit` fires once a drag, click, mark selection, or key
press lands. While a drag is active, the pointer takes precedence over `value`
and reconciles on release.

```tsx
const [span, setSpan] = useState<[number, number]>([25, 75]);

<RangeSpan
  label="Exposure window"
  value={span}
  onValueChange={setSpan}
  onValueCommit={saveWindow}
  step={5}
/>
```

### Minimum gap and marks

Set `minGap` to prevent the handles from getting closer than a given distance.
Pass `marks` as numbers or `{ value, label }` entries: handles gently stick to
marks within 6 px while dragging, and clicking a mark label moves the nearest
handle there.

```tsx
<RangeSpan
  label="Budget"
  min={0}
  max={1000}
  step={25}
  minGap={50}
  defaultValue={[200, 750]}
  marks={[
    { value: 0, label: "$0" },
    { value: 250, label: "$250" },
    { value: 500, label: "$500" },
    { value: 750, label: "$750" },
    { value: 1000, label: "$1k" },
  ]}
  format={(value) => `$${value}`}
/>
```

### Live label modes

Choose `liveLabels` to match your layout density:

- `"floating"` (default): renders live min and max pills directly above each
  handle and merges them automatically when handles converge.
- `"auto"`: shows the live `min – max` readout in the header row at rest and
  reveals floating pills on hover, keyboard focus, or drag.
- `"header"`: keeps the live min and max readout exclusively in the header row
  with no floating pills.

```tsx
<RangeSpan
  label="Active hours"
  liveLabels="auto"
  min={0}
  max={24}
  step={0.5}
  minGap={2}
  defaultValue={[9, 17]}
  format={(v) => `${String(Math.floor(v)).padStart(2, "0")}:${v % 1 ? "30" : "00"}`}
/>
```

### Native forms

Pass `name` to render two hidden `<input type="hidden" name={name} />` elements
for the lower and upper bounds, readable via `formData.getAll(name)` in native
forms or Server Actions.

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag a handle | Moves that handle 1:1 from the exact point grabbed |
| Drag the selected fill | Pans the entire `[min, max]` span while preserving its width (`draggableSpan`) |
| Click or tap track | Jumps the nearest handle to that point and begins dragging |
| Drag past `min` or `max` | Stretches the track and fill with resistance; springs back on release |
| Push into `minGap` | Applies subtle resistance nudge and muted thunk without crossing |
| Click a mark label | Moves the nearest handle to that mark |
| `→` / `↑` | Increases the focused handle by `step` |
| `←` / `↓` | Decreases the focused handle by `step` |
| `Shift` + arrow, `PageUp` / `PageDown` | Moves the focused handle by `largeStep` (defaults to a tenth of the range) |
| `Home` / `End` | Jumps the focused handle to its legal lower or upper limit |

On touch screens the root uses `touch-action: pan-y` and waits for horizontal
travel before claiming a drag, so vertical page scrolling is never interrupted.
Right-to-left layouts mirror drag coordinates, stretch direction, and arrow
keys. Multi-touch second fingers are ignored while a drag is active.

## Sound and haptics

Crossing a step or mark plays a soft tiks click paired with a `selection`
haptic, throttled to one cue per 35 ms so fast sweeps never buzz. Ranges with
more than 40 steps tick every 10% instead of every step, and when `marks` are
provided only marks tick. Reaching `min` or `max` plays a slightly brighter
click, and pulling past an outer limit or pushing into `minGap` plays a single
muted thunk with a `rigid` haptic.

Sound and haptics fire only on user interaction — never on initial mount,
server rendering, controlled prop updates, or while the tab is hidden. Pass
`sound={false}` or `haptics={false}` to turn each off independently.

## Accessibility

Range Span wraps both handles in `role="group"` labelled by the visible `label`
(or visually hidden via `hideLabel`). Each handle is an independent
`role="slider"` button with `aria-label` (defaulting to `"Minimum <label>"` and
`"Maximum <label>"`, customizable via `minLabel` and `maxLabel`), dynamic
`aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` bounds respecting `minGap`, `aria-valuenow`,
and `aria-valuetext` formatted through `format`.

Both handles provide a 44×44 px hit target and render in an unclipped sibling
layer so the `focus-visible` ring is never clipped at `0%` or `100%`. Keyboard
adjustments update immediately with zero animation lag.

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, track stretching, thickening springs,
collision nudges, spring glides, and rolling digits are disabled in favor of
direct updates while keeping subtle opacity and color feedback. With
`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, floating pills drop backdrop blur for
opaque backgrounds.

## FAQ

### How do I install the Range Span component with shadcn?

Run `npx shadcn@latest add @tentui/range-span` after adding the `@tentui`
registry to `components.json` ([installation guide](/docs/installation)), or run
`npx shadcn@latest add https://tentui.com/r/range-span.json` directly. The CLI
copies `range-span.tsx` into your project and installs `motion`,
`@number-flow/react`, `web-haptics`, and `@rexa-developer/tiks`.

### How do the floating min and max labels avoid overlapping?

Range Span observes the track and label widths with `ResizeObserver` and clamps
each pill within the track bounds so neither overflows at the edges. When the
two pills get within 6 px of each other, they crossfade into a single centered
`min – max` pill and split back apart once they clear 12 px of separation.

### Is Range Span keyboard and screen-reader accessible?

Yes. It follows the WAI-ARIA multi-thumb slider pattern with a `role="group"`
wrapper and two independent `role="slider"` handles named by `minLabel` and
`maxLabel` (defaulting to `"Minimum <label>"` and `"Maximum <label>"`). Both
handles support arrow keys, `Shift` + arrows, `PageUp`, `PageDown`, `Home`, and
`End`, with instant keyboard updates and unclipped `focus-visible` rings.

### How do I use Range Span with React Hook Form or native forms?

Pass `field.value` and `field.onChange` from a React Hook Form `Controller` to
`value` and `onValueChange`, or pass `onValueCommit` to update form state only
when a drag or key press settles. For native forms or Server Actions, pass
`name` to emit two hidden inputs for the lower and upper bounds.

### Can I disable span panning, rubber-band stretch, or sound and haptics?

Yes. Pass `draggableSpan={false}` to turn off dragging the selected fill bar,
`maxStretch={0}` to disable rubber-band overdrag, and `sound={false}` or
`haptics={false}` to turn off audio and haptic cues independently.

### Is the Range Span component free to use?

Yes. Range Span is free and source-available: the shadcn CLI copies its source
into your project, and you can use it in unlimited personal and client projects
under the [TentUI License](/license). The license doesn't allow redistributing
or reselling the component itself.
