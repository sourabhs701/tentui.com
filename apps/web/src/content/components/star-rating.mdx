## Features

- Rates with a tap, a press-and-sweep across the row, or the arrow keys, and
  previews the value under a mouse before you click.
- Takes half stars with `allowHalf`: the left half of a star gives x.5.
- Plays a click and a selection haptic for each star crossed, throttled to one
  per 35 ms, then a pop and a success haptic when the pick lands, or a success
  chime when you give full marks.
- Slides each star's fill to the pointer, lifts newly filled stars with a
  staggered spring, and drains them right to left when cleared.
- Clears when you tap the current value again, with `Backspace`, or with
  `Delete`. Turn this off with `clearable={false}`.
- Leaves vertical swipes to the page, so it can sit in a scrolling list on
  touch screens.
- Shows a caption per step with `labels`, submits with native forms through
  `name`, and has a read-only mode for review cards and product lists.

## Usage

```tsx
import { StarRating } from "@/components/star-rating";
```

```tsx
<StarRating
  aria-label="Rate your stay"
  allowHalf
  labels={["Poor", "Fair", "Good", "Great", "Excellent"]}
/>
```

Use `value` and `onValueChange` to control it. `onValueChange` fires on every
change, including each step of a sweep; `onValueCommit` fires once a pick
lands: when a press is released, a key moves the value, or the value is
cleared. While a sweep is in progress the drag wins over the `value` prop.
Changes that come from the `value` prop stay silent.

```tsx
const [rating, setRating] = useState(0);

<StarRating value={rating} onValueChange={setRating} />
```

Set `readOnly` to show a rating without interaction. Read-only ratings show
any fraction, so `4.3` fills 30% of the fifth star.

```tsx
<StarRating value={4.5} readOnly size="sm" />
```

Set `max` for up to 10 stars, and `size` to `sm`, `md`, or `lg`. Give it a
`name` to submit the value with a native form; an empty rating submits an empty
string.

## Accessibility

The stars are a `radiogroup` named with `aria-label` (default "Rating"), with
one `radio` per step named "1 star", "2 stars", and so on, or "2.5 stars" with
`allowHalf`. Only the checked radio is in the tab order, and the focus ring
wraps the whole star. Read-only ratings are a single image named "Rated 4.5
out of 5". Each star is 44 px tall, so it's an easy touch target.

`←`/`→` and `↑`/`↓` move one step (half a star with `allowHalf`), `Home` and
`End` jump to the lowest and highest rating, `Space` picks the focused radio,
and `Backspace` or `Delete` clears the value when `clearable`. In right-to-left
layouts the stars fill from the right and `←`/`→` are mirrored.

With reduced motion enabled the fills change in place, with no rise, nudge,
press dip, or drain. Sound and haptics stay on because they confirm each star
rather than decorate movement; turn them off with `sound={false}` and
`haptics={false}`.
