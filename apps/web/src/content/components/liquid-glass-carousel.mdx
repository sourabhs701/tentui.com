## Features

- The edge of the frame is the effect. Inside the outer `meltZone` at each
  end, a lens fixed to the frame magnifies cards toward the edge, so they
  smear into long streaks, flare wider like a drip, and pick up a thin rainbow
  fringe where the colour channels bend by different amounts. Cards coming
  back in contract into a clean capsule.
- The melt is a function of position only, so it follows a drag exactly and
  reverses exactly. The band between the zones is passed through untouched,
  so the centred card is pixel-crisp.
- Slides are real DOM: images, video, text, or anything else. There is no
  WebGL and no canvas. The lens is one SVG filter on the track, and its
  displacement maps are drawn once per size.
- A real carousel underneath: drag or swipe with momentum and snap, wheel and
  trackpad, arrow keys, `Home`, `End`, `PageUp`, and `PageDown`, and clicking a
  neighbour centres it.
- Loops seamlessly by default, with clones drawn only when there are too few
  slides to hide the seam. Optional autoplay, with a pause button.
- A soft glass tick and a light haptic when a slide you moved to settles.
- At rest nothing renders per frame, and no animation loop runs.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  LiquidGlassCarousel,
  LiquidGlassCarouselSlide,
} from "@/components/liquid-glass-carousel";
```

```tsx
<LiquidGlassCarousel aria-label="Featured artists">
  {artists.map((artist) => (
    <LiquidGlassCarouselSlide key={artist.id} aria-label={artist.name}>
      <img src={artist.portrait} alt="" className="size-full object-cover" />
    </LiquidGlassCarouselSlide>
  ))}
</LiquidGlassCarousel>
```

Slides are capsules by default. Pass a `className` such as `rounded-3xl` to
change the shape, and `slideSize` and `gap` to change the size. Px sizes scale
down to fit narrow containers. The viewport is 2.6 slides long; set the
`--lgc-length` CSS variable on the root to change it.

Add `LiquidGlassCarouselControls` anywhere inside the carousel for previous
and next buttons and a "03 / 07" counter. It also shows the pause button when
autoplay is on.

```tsx
<LiquidGlassCarousel aria-label="Swatches" autoplay={2200} className="gap-2">
  {slides}
  <LiquidGlassCarouselControls />
</LiquidGlassCarousel>
```

Use `index` and `onIndexChange` to control it, or a ref for `next()`,
`prev()`, and `goTo(index)`. Controlled changes and ref calls glide there
silently; a jump of several slides skips ahead and glides the last two.

```tsx
const [index, setIndex] = useState(0);

<LiquidGlassCarousel
  aria-label="Chapters"
  index={index}
  onIndexChange={setIndex}
>
  {slides}
</LiquidGlassCarousel>
```

Set `orientation="horizontal"` for a row, which follows the page direction in
right-to-left layouts. `melt="off"` keeps the soft fade at the edges but never
distorts the slides.

The fringe is mixed against the page colour, `--background`. On another
surface, such as a card, set `--lgc-surface` on the root to that colour.

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag or swipe along the axis | Moves the slides 1:1; letting go snaps to the nearest slide, and a flick travels up to two |
| Wheel or trackpad | One slide per gesture |
| Click or tap a neighbour | Centres it |
| `ArrowDown` / `ArrowUp` | Next / previous slide (vertical) |
| `ArrowRight` / `ArrowLeft` | Next / previous slide (horizontal; swapped in right-to-left pages) |
| `PageDown` / `PageUp` | Three slides forward / back |
| `Home` / `End` | First / last slide |

Fast key repeats queue up to three slides ahead, onto one spring. A swipe
across the axis still scrolls the page. Without `loop`, a drag rubber-bands
past either end and the wheel scrolls the page on once you reach an end.

## Sound and haptics

Each time a slide you moved to settles at the centre, it plays a short, bright
glass tick, and on touch devices a light `selection` haptic. Autoplay steps,
the first render, `index` changes, and ref calls are silent, and nothing plays
while the tab is hidden. Turn each off with `sound={false}` and
`haptics={false}`.

## Browser support

The lens needs SVG filters on HTML that are fast and exact, so it runs in
Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Arc, Opera). Safari draws SVG filters on HTML
unreliably and Firefox draws them on the CPU, so there each card stretches
toward the edge, flares a little, and softens with a small blur instead. Both
fade into the page at the very edge.

## Accessibility

The root is a `region` with `aria-roledescription="carousel"` and your
`aria-label`. Each slide is a `group` with `aria-roledescription="slide"`,
labelled "n of N" unless you pass `aria-label`. Only the centred slide is in
the tab order and the accessibility tree; its neighbours and the loop's clones
are `inert`.

The slides take keyboard focus as one stop, with a focus ring for keyboard
focus only. A polite live region announces "Slide n of N" when you move the
carousel, but not on autoplay steps. Buttons have 44 px hit areas.

Autoplay pauses while the pointer is over the carousel, while it has keyboard
focus, during a press, and while the tab is hidden. A visible pause button
stops it for good (WCAG 2.2.2).

With reduced motion enabled the slides never melt (the edges keep their soft
fade), each move is a 150 ms fade instead of a glide, and autoplay starts
paused until the play button is pressed. Sound and haptics stay on because
they confirm the move rather than decorate it.

## FAQ

### How do I install the Liquid Glass Carousel with shadcn?

Run `npx shadcn@latest add @tentui/liquid-glass-carousel` after adding the
`@tentui` registry to `components.json` ([installation guide](/docs/installation)).
The CLI copies `liquid-glass-carousel.tsx` into your project and installs
`motion`, `lucide-react`, `web-haptics`, and `@rexa-developer/tiks`.

### Is the carousel accessible, and can autoplay be paused?

Yes. It is a labelled `region` with slides as labelled groups, arrow keys,
`Home`, `End`, `PageUp`, and `PageDown` move it, and a polite live region
announces changes you make. Autoplay pauses on hover, keyboard focus, and a
hidden tab, and always shows a pause button.

### Does the melt work in Safari and Firefox, and is it fast?

The full lens, with streaks and the rainbow fringe, runs in Chromium browsers
as one SVG filter on the track, with its maps drawn once per size. Safari and
Firefox get a lighter melt instead: cards stretch toward the edge, flare a
little, blur slightly, and fade into the page. At rest nothing renders per
frame in either.

### What happens with reduced motion?

The slides never distort, each move becomes a 150 ms fade instead of a glide,
and autoplay starts paused. The edges keep their soft fade.

### Can I use images, video, or any content in the slides?

Yes. Slides are ordinary DOM elements, so any children work, and the melt is
applied to the whole track as it is drawn. Use `horizontal` orientation for a
row of wider cards.

### Is the Liquid Glass Carousel free to use?

Yes. The Liquid Glass Carousel is free and source-available: the shadcn CLI
copies its source into your project, and you can use it in unlimited personal
and client projects under the [TentUI License](/license).
