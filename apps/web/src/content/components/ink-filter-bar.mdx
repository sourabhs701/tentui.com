## Features

- Filter chips beside a solid "24 results" pill. Pick a chip and a bead of
  ink buds from the pill, joins the chip through a short liquid neck, and
  floods it from the edge nearest the pill while the chip slides in and docks.
  The pill and the picked chips end up as one capsule: "17 results | Cycling".
- Deselect and the chip tears free: the neck stretches and snaps, a small bead
  shrinks back into the chip's edge, and the chip drains back to an outline in
  its own slot.
- The label is drawn twice, outline color underneath and inverted on top,
  clipped by the same edge as the ink. Text stays crisp and readable mid-flood,
  on any background.
- The count rolls one digit at a time, each a beat after the last, starting
  with the flood.
- Hovering a chip with a mouse makes its outline wobble very slightly, like a
  wet edge.
- `type="single"` swaps the picked chip for the new one in one motion, and an
  optional clear button at the end of the capsule drains every chip back.
- The row scrolls sideways with soft edge fades, or wraps with `wrap`.
- Monochrome by design: the ink is your `foreground` color, so it's near-black
  in light mode and near-white in dark mode.
- At rest the capsule is plain CSS. The goo filter, the render loop, and the
  wobble filter only exist while something moves.

## Usage

```tsx
import { InkFilterBar } from "@/components/ink-filter-bar";
```

The bar shows whatever `count` you pass, so compute it from the same data
the filter narrows. `value` lists the picked chips in the order they joined
the capsule.

```tsx
const [sports, setSports] = useState<string[]>([]);
const count = clubs.filter((club) =>
  sports.every((sport) => club.sports.includes(sport)),
).length;

<InkFilterBar
  aria-label="Filter clubs by sport"
  options={[
    { value: "cycling", label: "Cycling" },
    { value: "tennis", label: "Tennis" },
    { value: "running", label: "Running" },
  ]}
  value={sports}
  onValueChange={setSports}
  count={count}
/>
```

Use `type="single"` for a category picker. Picking another chip detaches the
old one and absorbs the new one together, and picking the same chip again
clears it. Give an option a `count` to show its own total after the label:

```tsx
<InkFilterBar
  type="single"
  aria-label="Template category"
  options={[
    { value: "landing", label: "Landing", count: 18 },
    { value: "dashboard", label: "Dashboard", count: 9 },
  ]}
  value={category}
  onValueChange={setCategory}
  count={total}
  label="templates"
/>
```

Pass `onClear` to show a clear button at the end of the capsule. It clears
the selection through `onValueChange`, then calls `onClear`. Use `wrap` when
there are more chips than fit on one line, and `formatCount` for another
locale or a capped number:

```tsx
<InkFilterBar
  wrap
  aria-label="Filter places by cuisine"
  options={cuisines}
  value={picked}
  onValueChange={setPicked}
  onClear={() => track("filters_cleared")}
  count={places.length}
  label="places"
  formatCount={(count) => (count > 999 ? "999+" : String(count))}
/>
```

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click or tap a chip | Absorbs it into the capsule, or detaches it |
| `Tab` | Moves into the chips, then to the clear button |
| `←` / `→` | Moves between chips in the order they're drawn |
| `Home` / `End` | Moves to the first or last chip |
| `Space` / `Enter` | Toggles the focused chip |
| Clear button | Drains every chip back, one after another |

A chip keeps focus as it moves between the capsule and the row. Picking a
chip while the row is scrolled scrolls back to the start, so the capsule
stays in view.

## Sound and haptics

Absorbing a chip plays a soft, wet drop and a `light` haptic. Detaching one,
or clearing, plays a lighter unstick and a `selection` haptic. There is one
sound and one haptic per toggle, and nothing on hover.

Sound and haptics only follow user input. The first render and changes to the
`value` or `count` props are silent, and nothing plays while the tab is
hidden. Turn each off with `sound={false}` and `haptics={false}`.

## Accessibility

The bar is a `group` named by the required `aria-label`, and each chip is a
`button` with `aria-pressed`, so a screen reader hears "Cycling, toggle
button, pressed". The chips share one tab stop with a roving `tabIndex`. The
count is read from a polite live region, such as "17 results", after the
selection settles, so a quick run of toggles is announced once. The ink,
neck, and inverted label are hidden from assistive technology, and the
visible label is never hidden.

Chips are 36 px tall with a 44 px hit area. The focus ring shows for keyboard
focus only and sits outside the ink, so it's visible on outlined and filled
chips. The label keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast on the page and on the ink, in
light and dark mode. In right-to-left layouts the capsule sits on the right
and the ink floods from each chip's right edge.

With reduced motion enabled there is no goo, neck, or wobble: chips move to
their new place at once and fade in there, and the count swaps with a short
fade. Sound and haptics stay on because they confirm the toggle rather than
decorate movement.

## FAQ

### How do I install the Ink Filter Bar with shadcn?

Run `npx shadcn@latest add @tentui/ink-filter-bar` after adding the `@tentui`
registry to `components.json` ([installation guide](/docs/installation)), or run
`npx shadcn@latest add https://tentui.com/r/ink-filter-bar.json` without it. The
CLI copies `ink-filter-bar.tsx` into your project and installs `motion`,
`web-haptics`, and `@rexa-developer/tiks`.

### Can users pick more than one filter?

Yes. The default `type="multiple"` lets any number of chips join the capsule,
in the order they were picked. Set `type="single"` for a category or sort
picker: picking a new chip detaches the old one and absorbs the new one in one
motion.

### How do I wire the count to real data?

The bar only shows the `count` you pass. Keep the selection in state with
`value` and `onValueChange`, and compute `count` from the same filtered list or
from your API's total. The digits roll whenever the number changes, and the
live region announces it once the selection settles.

### Is the Ink Filter Bar accessible?

Yes. It is a `group` named by `aria-label`, each chip is a toggle `button` with
`aria-pressed`, arrow keys plus `Home` and `End` move between chips, and the
result count is announced through a polite live region. Focus stays on a chip
while it moves into or out of the capsule.

### Does it respect reduced motion, and can I turn off sound and haptics?

Yes. With `prefers-reduced-motion` the ink, neck, and wobble are skipped:
chips fade in at their new place and the count swaps with a short fade. Sound
and haptics only follow user input; turn them off with `sound={false}` and
`haptics={false}`.

### Is the Ink Filter Bar free to use?

Yes. The Ink Filter Bar is free and source-available: the shadcn CLI copies its
source into your project, and you can use it in unlimited personal and client
projects under the [TentUI License](/license). The license doesn't allow
