Share Sheet is a compact React share menu for shadcn/ui built for destinations,
one-tap link copying, and secondary actions, distinct from a full-page bottom
sheet. On desktop it springs from its trigger as an origin-aware floating card;
on mobile viewports it rises as a compact floating bottom tray you can flick to
dismiss. TentUI is free and source-available, so installing it copies the full
component source into your project.

## Features

- Adapts automatically (`placement="auto"`) between an origin-aware floating
  popover on desktop (`>= 640px`) and a compact floating bottom tray on narrow
  mobile screens, or locks to `"popover"` or `"sheet"`.
- Measures the trigger's exact anchor point to project `transform-origin`,
  auto-flips between `top` and `bottom` when viewport room is tight, and clamps
  within `collisionPadding`.
- Ships a built-in `ShareSheetLink` bar with one-tap clipboard copy, blur-masked
  `Copy` to `Copied` morph with zero layout shift, and a `document.execCommand`
  fallback.
- Pairs a horizontally scrollable `ShareSheetTargets` rail (with dynamic edge
  masks, roving arrow-key focus, and inline delivery checkmarks) with grouped
  `ShareSheetActions` rows supporting shortcuts, toggle states, and inline
  confirmations.
- Tracks 1:1 vertical drag-to-dismiss with upward rubber-banding and release
  velocity projection in mobile sheet mode without blocking horizontal target
  scrolling.
- Includes three restrained surface treatments (`surface`, `glass`, `minimal`),
  subtle Web Audio ticks and haptics (`sound` and `haptics` props), and full
  `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency` fallbacks.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  ShareSheet,
  ShareSheetAction,
  ShareSheetActions,
  ShareSheetContent,
  ShareSheetFooter,
  ShareSheetHeader,
  ShareSheetLink,
  ShareSheetTarget,
  ShareSheetTargets,
  ShareSheetTrigger,
} from "@/components/share-sheet";
```

You can also access every primitive via dot notation on `ShareSheet` (for
example `ShareSheet.Trigger`, `ShareSheet.Content`, `ShareSheet.Targets`).

```tsx
<ShareSheet
  url="https://tentui.com/components/share-sheet"
  title="TentUI Share Sheet"
>
  <ShareSheetTrigger>Share</ShareSheetTrigger>
  <ShareSheetContent>
    <ShareSheetHeader subtitle="tentui.com/components/share-sheet" />
    <ShareSheetLink />
    <ShareSheetTargets>
      <ShareSheetTarget label="Slack" icon={<SlackIcon />} />
      <ShareSheetTarget label="Mail" icon={<MailIcon />} />
    </ShareSheetTargets>
    <ShareSheetActions>
      <ShareSheetAction feedbackLabel="Copied" onSelect={copyMarkdown}>
        Copy Markdown
      </ShareSheetAction>
    </ShareSheetActions>
  </ShareSheetContent>
</ShareSheet>
```

### Controlled state

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to control visibility from parent state, and set
`closeOnSelect` if you want selecting a link, destination, or action to close
the sheet after its confirmation beat.

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<ShareSheet
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  closeOnSelect
  url="https://tentui.com/components/share-sheet"
  title="Design Spec"
>
  {/* ... */}
</ShareSheet>
```

### Surface variants and placement

Switch between `variant="surface"` (default), `variant="glass"` (translucent
vibrancy material), and `variant="minimal"` (flat hairline border). Override
`placement` with `"popover"` or `"sheet"` when you want a fixed presentation
across breakpoints.

### Inside a container

Pass `container` to `ShareSheetContent` to portal and position the share sheet
inside a specific frame or preview card instead of the viewport, paired with
`modal={false}` when surrounding UI should remain interactive.

## Accessibility

`ShareSheetContent` renders `role="dialog"` wired to `ShareSheetHeader` via
`aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby`, with a polite `aria-live` region that
announces copy and target confirmations. In modal mode (`modal={true}`), `Tab`
and `Shift+Tab` stay trapped inside the sheet, `Escape` closes it and returns
focus to `ShareSheetTrigger`, and pointer opens keep focus rings hidden until
keyboard navigation begins.

Inside `ShareSheetTargets`, `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Home`, and `End` move
between destinations. Inside `ShareSheetActions`, `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown`
move between action rows, and toggle actions expose `aria-pressed`. With
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, spring and blur entrances collapse to a 120 ms
opacity cross-fade; with `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, the `glass`
surface switches to an opaque background.

## FAQ

### How do I install the Share Sheet with shadcn?

Run `npx shadcn@latest add @tentui/share-sheet` after adding the `@tentui`
registry to `components.json` ([installation guide](/docs/installation)), or run
`npx shadcn@latest add https://tentui.com/r/share-sheet.json` directly. The CLI
copies `share-sheet.tsx` into your project and installs `@rexa-developer/tiks`,
`lucide-react`, `motion`, and `web-haptics`.

### How is Share Sheet different from Snap Sheet?

Share Sheet is a compact, purpose-built overlay for share destinations, link
copying, and quick actions rather than a multi-detent full-page drawer. On
desktop it anchors directly to the Share trigger as an origin-aware floating
card, and on mobile it rises as a compact floating tray sized to its content.

### Is the Share Sheet accessible with keyboard and screen readers?

Yes. `ShareSheetContent` uses `role="dialog"`, links its title and subtitle via
`aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby`, traps focus in modal mode, closes on
`Escape` while returning focus to the trigger, and announces copy and target
confirmations via an `aria-live="polite"` region. Arrow keys navigate across
`ShareSheetTargets` and `ShareSheetActions`.

### Can I control the open state and customize the appearance?

Yes. Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` for controlled visibility, `closeOnSelect`
to auto-dismiss after a selection, `placement` (`"auto"`, `"popover"`, or
`"sheet"`), and `variant` (`"surface"`, `"glass"`, or `"minimal"`). Every slot
also accepts a standard `className` prop for Tailwind styling.

### Is the Share Sheet free to use?

Yes. Share Sheet is free and source-available: the shadcn CLI copies its source
into your project, and you can use it in unlimited personal and client projects
under the [TentUI License](/license). The license doesn't allow redistributing
or reselling the component itself.
