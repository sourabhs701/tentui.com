## Features

- Clear plastic film sealed to a tray. Each dome is see-through, with a thin
  rim, one crisp highlight, and light pooling on the tray beneath it. Popped
  bubbles become cloudy, wrinkled discs of slack film. Tint the film `blue` or
  `pink`, or leave it `clear`.
- Press a bubble to pop it, or hold and sweep across the sheet to pop every
  bubble you cross. The path between pointer events is tested too, so fast
  flicks skip nothing.
- Each pop is a quick squash with a slight twist, then the dome gives way and
  a pressure wave runs out through the film. Once the sheet is clear it
  re-inflates after a short pause, in a ripple from the last bubble popped:
  each bubble blooms just past full and settles dead still.
- Every pop is a short sound that drops in pitch, a little higher or lower
  each time, with a light haptic tick. Clearing the sheet plays a soft, low
  flourish with a firmer pulse.
- At rest nothing animates, so the sheet is perfectly still.

## Usage

```tsx
import { BubbleWrap } from "@/components/bubble-wrap";
```

```tsx
<BubbleWrap />
```

The sheet fills the width of its container, up to the width at which each
bubble is 42 px. On narrower containers fewer columns fit, so bubbles wrap
onto more rows and never shrink below 36 px. Popped bubbles keep their state
by index when the layout or size changes. Set a different width with
`className`, for example `max-w-[400px]`.

### Blue tint with a count

`showCount` shows how many bubbles are popped under the sheet. The count also
names the sheet for screen readers.

```tsx
<BubbleWrap tint="blue" rows={4} showCount />
```

### Sound off

Turn the sound off and keep the haptics, or the other way round:

```tsx
<BubbleWrap sound={false} />
```

### Pops, clears and resets

`onPop` fires once for each bubble that pops, and `onClear` once when the last
one pops. With `autoReset={false}` the sheet stays flat until you call
`reset()` on a ref. `popAll()` pops everything in a ripple.

```tsx
const sheet = useRef<BubbleWrapHandle>(null);

<BubbleWrap
  ref={sheet}
  autoReset={false}
  onPop={(index) => console.log("popped", index)}
  onClear={() => console.log("clear")}
/>
<button onClick={() => sheet.current?.reset()}>Reset</button>
```

`defaultPopped` starts some bubbles flat, for example
`defaultPopped={[3, 8, 14]}`.

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Press a bubble | Pops it |
| Hold and sweep | Pops every bubble crossed |
| `Arrow` keys | Move between bubbles |
| `Home` / `End` | First or last bubble |
| `Space` / `Enter` | Pops the focused bubble |
| **Pop all** button | Pops every bubble in a ripple |

A popped bubble stays flat until the sheet resets, and pressing it again does
nothing. Only one pointer sweeps at a time: a second finger and a right click
are ignored. Lifting or losing the pointer, or moving it off the sheet, ends
the sweep.

On touch screens the sheet sets `touch-action: none`, so a swipe that starts
on it pops bubbles rather than scrolling the page. Scroll by swiping outside
the sheet.

## Sound and haptics

Each pop is a sine thump that falls from about 180 Hz to 70 Hz in 80 ms,
under a few milliseconds of filtered noise, with the pitch varied by up to
12% either way. Pops are spaced at least 16 ms apart, at most six ring at
once, and a limiter sits on the mix, so even a fast sweep never clips or
crackles. Each pop also triggers a `light` haptic, at most every 35 ms.
Clearing the sheet plays a soft, low falling tick once, with a `medium`
haptic.

The audio context is created on the first press. Sound and haptics only
follow user input. Mounting the sheet, re-inflating it, `reset()`, and
`popAll()` are silent, and nothing plays while the tab is hidden. Turn each
off with `sound={false}` and `haptics={false}`.

## Accessibility

The sheet is a `group` named "Bubble wrap", followed by the count when
`showCount` is on. Pass `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` to name it
yourself. Each bubble is a real toggle button labelled like "Bubble 3 of 30",
with `aria-pressed` set once it's popped.

The bubbles are a single tab stop with a roving `tabindex`, and the arrow
keys move between them. Sweeping is a path-based gesture, so pressing each
bubble on its own, the keyboard, and the **Pop all** button are its
single-pointer equivalents
([WCAG 2.5.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pointer-gestures.html)).
**Pop all** follows the bubbles in the tab order and only shows while it has
keyboard focus.

A polite live region announces how many bubbles are left each time the
count passes a multiple of five,
"All popped" when the sheet is clear, and "New sheet" when it refills. The
focus ring, a dark ring with a white gap that reads on any surface, shows
for keyboard focus only.

With reduced motion enabled there is no squash, pressure wave, or ripple: popped
bubbles swap instantly and the sheet refills with a quick fade. Sound and
haptics still follow their props, because they confirm the pop rather than
decorate movement.
