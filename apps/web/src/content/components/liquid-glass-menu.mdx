## Features

- A real [Base UI](https://base-ui.com/react/components/menu) menu underneath:
  roles, focus management, typeahead, `Esc`, outside clicks, radio and
  checkbox items all come from Base UI, not from this component.
- Press the round glass button and it flows into the menu. The panel springs
  out of the button with a little overshoot, joined to it by a gooey neck for
  the first few frames, and the rows sharpen from a soft blur one after
  another. Close it and the panel drains back into the button.
- Frosted glass in light and dark: the page behind is blurred and saturated
  through a translucent fill, under a bright rim and a soft shadow. In
  Chromium the backdrop also bends at the rim, like it would through a lens.
- The highlight is a small glass lens that slides between rows on a spring,
  following the pointer and the arrow keys. The highlighted row's icon also
  grows and its label darkens, so the lens is never the only cue.
- A glass pop on open, a lower one on close, a tick on select, and matching
  haptics on supported phones.
- At rest nothing runs: no SVG filter, no animation frame, and no
  `will-change`. The goo filter only exists while the menu opens or closes.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  LiquidGlassMenu,
  LiquidGlassMenuContent,
  LiquidGlassMenuItem,
  LiquidGlassMenuTrigger,
} from "@/components/liquid-glass-menu";
```

```tsx
<LiquidGlassMenu>
  <LiquidGlassMenuTrigger aria-label="Add" />
  <LiquidGlassMenuContent side="top" align="end">
    <LiquidGlassMenuItem icon={<CameraIcon />} onClick={openCamera}>
      Camera
    </LiquidGlassMenuItem>
    <LiquidGlassMenuItem icon={<MicIcon />}>Audio</LiquidGlassMenuItem>
  </LiquidGlassMenuContent>
</LiquidGlassMenu>
```

The default trigger is a 44 px round glass button with a plus and no text, so
it needs an `aria-label`. With it, the panel opens over the button and grows
out of it. To use your own trigger, pass an element to `render` and the label
as children. The panel then sits `sideOffset` away from it and still grows out
of it.

```tsx
<LiquidGlassMenuTrigger render={<Button variant="outline" />}>
  Sort
</LiquidGlassMenuTrigger>
```

Groups, labels, separators, radio items, and checkbox items work like their
Base UI counterparts. `shortcut` shows a hint at the end of a row; it doesn't
bind the key.

```tsx
<LiquidGlassMenuContent align="end">
  <LiquidGlassMenuGroup>
    <LiquidGlassMenuLabel>Sort by</LiquidGlassMenuLabel>
    <LiquidGlassMenuRadioGroup value={sort} onValueChange={setSort}>
      <LiquidGlassMenuRadioItem value="newest" shortcut="⌘1">
        Newest
      </LiquidGlassMenuRadioItem>
      <LiquidGlassMenuRadioItem value="name" shortcut="⌘2">
        Name
      </LiquidGlassMenuRadioItem>
    </LiquidGlassMenuRadioGroup>
  </LiquidGlassMenuGroup>
  <LiquidGlassMenuSeparator />
  <LiquidGlassMenuCheckboxItem checked={hidden} onCheckedChange={setHidden}>
    Show hidden
  </LiquidGlassMenuCheckboxItem>
</LiquidGlassMenuContent>
```

`open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, and `modal` are Base UI's root props.
`morph={false}` swaps the blob for a plain scale-and-fade popover, and
`refraction="off"` keeps the glass a clean blur everywhere.

```tsx
<LiquidGlassMenu morph={false} sound={false}>
```

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click or tap the trigger | Opens the menu |
| `Enter`, `Space`, or `ArrowDown` on the trigger | Opens it and highlights the first item |
| `ArrowUp` / `ArrowDown` | Moves the lens to the previous or next item |
| `Home` / `End` | Moves the lens to the first or last item |
| Type a letter | Jumps to the next item starting with it |
| `Enter` / `Space`, click, or tap an item | Chooses it |
| `Esc` or a click outside | Closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger |

A press on the trigger can be dragged onto an item and released to choose it,
as in native menus. A long press released where the trigger was doesn't
choose the row that grew over it.

## Sound and haptics

Opening plays a short glass pop and a `light` haptic, closing plays a lower,
shorter pop and a `soft` haptic, and choosing an item plays a tick and a
`selection` haptic. Choosing an item that closes the menu plays only the tick.

Sound and haptics only follow user input. Mounting, `defaultOpen`, and changes
to the `open` prop are silent, and nothing plays while the tab is hidden. Turn
each off with `sound={false}` and `haptics={false}`.

## Accessibility

Base UI provides the semantics: the trigger is a button with
`aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`, the panel has `role="menu"`, and
rows are `menuitem`, `menuitemradio`, or `menuitemcheckbox`. The goo, glass,
and lens layers are hidden from assistive technology. The trigger's focus ring
shows for keyboard focus only, and the trigger and every row are at least
44 px tall.

The glass fill is opaque enough for text to keep a 4.5:1 contrast ratio over
any backdrop, light or dark, and secondary text such as shortcuts and labels
is drawn darker than the usual muted color for the same reason.

With reduced motion enabled there is no blob, neck, or blur: the panel fades
in and out over 120 ms and the lens jumps straight to each item. With reduced
transparency, or in browsers without `backdrop-filter`, the glass becomes a
solid `popover` surface with the same rim and shadow. Sound and haptics stay
on under reduced motion because they confirm actions rather than decorate
movement.

## FAQ

### How do I install the Liquid Glass Menu with shadcn?

Run `npx shadcn@latest add @tentui/liquid-glass-menu` after adding the
`@tentui` registry to `components.json` ([installation guide](/docs/installation)),
or run `npx shadcn@latest add https://tentui.com/r/liquid-glass-menu.json`
without it. The CLI copies `liquid-glass-menu.tsx` into your project and
installs `@base-ui/react`, `motion`, `web-haptics`, and `@rexa-developer/tiks`.

### Is the Liquid Glass Menu accessible?

Yes. It is a Base UI menu, so the trigger has `aria-haspopup` and
`aria-expanded`, the panel is a `role="menu"`, and arrow keys, `Home`, `End`,
typeahead, `Enter`, `Space`, and `Esc` all work, with focus returned to the
trigger on close. The highlight is never shown by the lens alone, and the
default trigger requires an `aria-label` in its types.

### Does the glass work in Safari and Firefox?

Yes. The frosted blur, rim, and shadow use `backdrop-filter`, which every
current browser supports. Only the refraction at the rim of the panel and the
lens needs Chromium, so Safari and Firefox get a clean blur instead, and
browsers without `backdrop-filter` get a solid surface.

### What happens with reduced motion or reduced transparency?

With `prefers-reduced-motion`, the morph, neck, and item blur are skipped:
the panel fades in over 120 ms and the lens moves instantly. With
`prefers-reduced-transparency`, the glass becomes a solid `popover` surface
with the same rim and shadow.

### Can I use my own trigger button?

Yes. Pass your element to `render` on `LiquidGlassMenuTrigger` and the label as
children, for example `render={<Button variant="outline" />}`. Your button
keeps its own styling and gets the menu behaviour, and the panel opens beside
it rather than over it.

### Is the Liquid Glass Menu free to use?

Yes. The Liquid Glass Menu is free and source-available: the shadcn CLI copies
its source into your project, and you can use it in unlimited personal and
client projects under the [TentUI License](/license). The license doesn't allow
redistributing or reselling the component itself.
