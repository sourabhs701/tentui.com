## Features

- Scrolls a tick strip under a fixed needle, with minor, major, and labelled
  ticks and faded edges.
- Coasts after a flick and lands on the nearest step with a spring; dragging
  past either end rubber-bands and springs back.
- Plays a click and a selection haptic for every step crossed, a stronger
  haptic on labelled ticks, and a pop at the bounds.
- Rolls the readout digits up or down in the direction you moved.
- Supports wheel and trackpad, tapping a tick, preset chips, double-click to
  reset, and full keyboard control.
- Moves with motion values and transforms only, and draws just the visible
  ticks on long ranges.

## Usage

```tsx
import { TickRuler } from "@/components/tick-ruler";
```

```tsx
<TickRuler
  label="Focus session length"
  defaultValue={25}
  max={90}
  labelEvery={15}
  unit="min"
  presets={[5, 15, 25, 45]}
/>
```

Use `value` and `onValueChange` to control it. `onValueChange` fires on every
step; `onValueCommit` fires once the ruler settles. Changes that come from the
`value` prop glide silently, and a drag in progress always wins.

```tsx
const [weight, setWeight] = useState(72.5);

<TickRuler
  label="Weight"
  value={weight}
  onValueChange={setWeight}
  min={30}
  max={200}
  step={0.5}
  majorEvery={2}
  labelEvery={10}
  unit="kg"
/>
```

Pass `format` to control the readout, chips, and `aria-valuetext`.

```tsx
<TickRuler
  label="Tip"
  defaultValue={18}
  max={40}
  format={(value) => `${value}%`}
/>
```

Set `orientation="vertical"` for height-style pickers, where higher values sit
above the needle, and `size` to `sm`, `md`, or `lg`.

## Accessibility

The ruler is a `slider` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`,
`aria-valuemax`, and an `aria-valuetext` built from `format` and `unit`. Name
it with `label`. Arrow keys move one step, `Shift` with an arrow or
`PageUp`/`PageDown` move ten, and `Home`/`End` jump to the bounds. The ticks
are hidden from assistive technology.

With reduced motion enabled there is no coast, rubber band, or digit roll, and
the strip jumps straight to each step. Sound and haptics stay on because they
confirm each step rather than decorate movement; turn them off with
`sound={false}` and `haptics={false}`.
