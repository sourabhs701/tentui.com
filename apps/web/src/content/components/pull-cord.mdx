## Features

- Drag the handle down and the beaded chain feeds out of the housing bead by
  bead. Each bead plays a soft tick with a haptic, and nudges the housing by a
  pixel.
- The handle pulls freely for `travel` px, then the chain resists. Hitting the
  end of travel plays a short, low thunk.
- Letting go past `threshold` flips the switch with a firm click, like a real
  lamp that clicks as you release. A short tug only wobbles the chain.
- On release the handle springs back with a little overshoot, and the chain
  swings like a pendulum that carries the release velocity, then settles.
- A click, tap, `Space`, or `Enter` plays the same tug: down past the
  threshold, flip, and back.
- The chain, handle, and swing move with motion values, so pulling never
  re-renders per frame.

## Usage

```tsx
import { PullCord } from "@/components/pull-cord";
```

```tsx
<PullCord label="Desk lamp" />
```

By default the chain hangs from a small lamp panel that lights up when the
switch is on. Pass `children` to use your own lit surface. The root carries
`data-state="checked"` or `"unchecked"` and the `group/pull-cord` class, so
the surface can style itself from it.

```tsx
<PullCord label="Night light" handle={<Star className="fill-current" />}>
  <div className="flex size-14 items-center justify-center rounded-full border bg-card group-data-[state=checked]/pull-cord:bg-amber-50">
    <Star className="size-5 text-zinc-300 group-data-[state=checked]/pull-cord:fill-amber-400 group-data-[state=checked]/pull-cord:text-amber-400" />
  </div>
</PullCord>
```

`variant="minimal"` shows only the housing, chain, and handle. Use `checked`
and `onCheckedChange` to let the parent react. Changing `checked` from the
parent updates the switch silently, with no sound or haptics.

```tsx
const [dark, setDark] = useState(false);

<PullCord
  variant="minimal"
  size="sm"
  label="Dark reading mode"
  checked={dark}
  onCheckedChange={setDark}
/>
```

`handle` replaces the default pull-bar, for example with an icon. Icons
without a `size-*` class are sized to match `size`. `chainLength` sets the
resting chain in px, so a shorter chain makes the whole switch shorter. The
component reserves room under the handle for a full pull, so it never
overlaps what comes next. Give the switch a `name` to submit `on` with a
native form while it's checked, like a checkbox.

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the handle down | Feeds the chain out, one tick per bead |
| Release past `threshold` | Flips the switch, then the chain swings back |
| Release before `threshold` | The chain wobbles back without flipping |
| Click or tap the handle | Plays a tug and flips the switch |
| `Space` / `Enter` | Plays a tug and flips the switch |

Pulling up does nothing; the handle stays at rest. After a flip the switch
re-arms once the handle is back within 4 px of rest, so a second pull or key
press while it's still springing back only wobbles the chain. A second finger
is ignored. A cancelled pointer, a window blur, or a hidden tab lets go of the
handle without flipping. In a right-to-left layout the chain swings the other
way first; nothing else changes.

## Sound and haptics

Each bead that comes out of the housing plays a soft tiks click with a
`selection` haptic, limited to one per 35 ms so a fast pull never buzzes.
Flipping on plays a deep, firm click, and flipping off a slightly higher one,
both with a `medium` haptic. Pulling into the end of travel plays a short low
thunk with a `rigid` haptic, once per pull.

Sound and haptics only follow user input. The first render, server rendering,
and changes to the `checked` prop are silent, and nothing plays while the tab
is hidden. `sound={false}` and `haptics={false}` turn each off independently.

## Accessibility

The handle is a `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`. It's named
by `label`, or by `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. With `showLabel`, the
label is shown under the cord and names the switch through
`aria-labelledby`. The housing, chain, and beads are hidden from assistive
technology. The focus ring shows only for keyboard focus, never after a click
or pull. The hit target is at least 44 px even though the handle is smaller.
A disabled switch is dimmed and can't be focused or pulled.

With reduced motion enabled, the chain doesn't swing and the handle eases
back without a spring or overshoot. A click or key press flips the switch
instantly, without the tug. Sound and haptics stay on because they confirm
input rather than decorate movement.
