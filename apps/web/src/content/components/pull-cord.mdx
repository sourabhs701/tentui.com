## Features

- A ceramic fixture, a steel ball chain, and a turned oak pull. The fixture's
  lens glows warm when the switch is on and casts a soft pool of light below.
- The chain is a small rope simulation with gravity and damping, run on a
  fixed timestep so it moves the same at 60 Hz and 120 Hz. Beads feed out of
  the fixture one at a time as you pull.
- Pull past the threshold and let go to flip the switch. The chain recoils,
  overshoots a little, and swings with the speed you let go at before it
  settles. A short tug only wobbles.
- Clicking the handle, or pressing `Space` or `Enter`, plays the same tug.
- Plays a soft tick and a selection haptic per bead, a firm click on the flip,
  and a low thunk at the end of the pull.
- At rest the chain is perfectly still, and nothing renders per frame. While
  it moves, React doesn't re-render: the beads and handle are moved directly.

## Usage

```tsx
import { PullCord } from "@/components/pull-cord";
```

```tsx
<PullCord label="Ceiling light" />
```

Use `checked` and `onCheckedChange` to control it. `onCheckedChange` fires
once per flip, the moment the handle is let go.

```tsx
const [lit, setLit] = useState(false);

<PullCord label="Reading lamp" checked={lit} onCheckedChange={setLit} />
```

Turn the sound off and keep the haptics, or the other way round:

```tsx
<PullCord label="Hallway light" sound={false} />
```

Give it a `name` to submit `"on"` or `"off"` with a native form. The component
is 200 px wide and 304 px tall, with room for the pull below the handle.

## Interaction

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the handle down | Feeds the chain out; past the threshold, letting go flips the switch |
| Drag sideways | The handle follows a little and swings on release |
| Click the handle | Tugs the chain and flips the switch |
| `Space` / `Enter` | Tugs the chain and flips the switch |

Past the end of its travel the chain rubber-bands. Losing the pointer, the
window, or the tab mid-pull lets go without flipping. A second finger is
ignored while the first one pulls.

## Sound and haptics

Each bead that leaves the fixture while you pull plays a soft metallic tick
with a `selection` haptic, throttled to one per 32 ms. The flip plays a firm
click with a `medium` haptic, a touch lower when turning on than off. Reaching
the end of the travel plays a low thunk with a `soft` haptic.

Sound and haptics only follow user input. The first render and changes to the
`checked` prop are silent, and nothing plays while the tab is hidden. Turn
each off with `sound={false}` and `haptics={false}`.

## Accessibility

The handle is a `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`, named by
`label`, `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby`. The fixture and chain are hidden
from assistive technology. The focus ring shows for keyboard focus only. The
handle's hit area is 48 by 64 px, and dragging it on a touch screen never
scrolls the page. A disabled pull cord is dimmed and can't be focused or
pulled.

With reduced motion enabled the chain doesn't swing or overshoot: a pulled
handle eases straight back, and a click or key press flips the light at once.
Sound and haptics stay on because they confirm the switch rather than
decorate movement.
